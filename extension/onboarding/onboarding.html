🎯 AI Conversation Control

The Recovery Layer for Generative AI

Control Your AI Workflow

AI conversations naturally degrade. AI Conversation Control turns your messages into recoverable checkpoints, ensuring you never lose a "good context" to hallucinations or drift.

What it does

📸

State Checkpoints

Your messages are automatically indexed as safe recovery points

🔍

Instant Search

Locate specific historical contexts across all your conversations

↩️

Jump Back

Instantly return to the original thread when the AI starts to drift

🔒

Privacy First

All data stays local on your device - nothing sent to the cloud

How to use

  1. Start a conversation on any supported platform
  2. Your messages are automatically saved as checkpoints
  3. Click the extension icon to view your checkpoints
  4. Search and jump back to any point in your conversation
