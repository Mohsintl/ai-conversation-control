🎯 AI Conversation Control

Never lose your best AI conversations

Welcome!

AI Conversation Control automatically captures your messages as checkpoints, giving you a safety net for long AI conversations.

What it does

📸

Automatic Checkpoints

Every message you send is automatically saved as a checkpoint

🔍

Search & Find

Quickly search through all your saved messages

↩️

Jump Back

Return to any checkpoint when the conversation drifts

🔒

Privacy First

All data stays local on your device - nothing sent to the cloud

How to use

  1. Start a conversation on any supported platform
  2. Your messages are automatically saved as checkpoints
  3. Click the extension icon to view your checkpoints
  4. Search and jump back to any point in your conversation
